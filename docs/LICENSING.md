# Data and software licensing

- OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL). Preserve attribution and comply with share-alike obligations for qualifying derivative databases.
- MapLibre libraries are open source; check the exact package version license before distribution.
- Valhalla and Photon are open-source projects; retain their notices and verify container/image licenses.
- Every bundled AI model and voice must be reviewed separately for weights license, code license, attribution, redistribution, commercial-use restrictions and acceptable-use terms.
- Do not redistribute regional map extracts, model weights or third-party assets until their terms have been checked.

This document is an engineering checklist, not legal advice.
